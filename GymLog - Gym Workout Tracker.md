# GymLog - Gym Workout Tracker

A simple, offline-first web app for tracking gym workouts on your iPhone or any device with a web browser.

## Features

- **Workout Logging** - Track exercises, sets, reps, and weight
- **Rest Timer** - Configurable rest timer between sets (30s - 3m) with haptic feedback
- **Workout History** - View all past workouts grouped by date
- **Progress Charts** - Track your personal best for each exercise over time
- **Workout Templates** - Save routines and start from templates
- **Bodyweight Support** - Log reps-only for pull-ups, dips, push-ups, etc.
- **Local Storage** - All data stays on your device (no account needed)
- **Offline** - Works completely offline

## How to Use

1. Open [GymLog](https://yourusername.github.io/gymlog-web) in Safari on your iPhone
2. Tap "Start Workout" to begin logging
3. Add exercises from the library or create custom ones
4. Log sets with weight and reps
5. Use the rest timer between sets
6. Finish your workout to save it
7. View history and progress charts

## Installation

### On iPhone
1. Open the link in Safari
2. Tap the Share button
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Now you have an app icon on your home screen!

### Self-Hosting
Just deploy the `index.html` file to any web server or GitHub Pages.

## Data Storage

All data is stored in your browser's local storage. Your data never leaves your device.

## Technologies

- Vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript
- No dependencies
- Works on all modern browsers
